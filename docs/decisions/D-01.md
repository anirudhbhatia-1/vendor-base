# D-01 — Launch neighbourhood and vendor cohort

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Accountable owner:** Operations Lead  
**Due date:** 20 August 2026  
**Blocks:** Phase 1 pilot recruitment, Phase 0.2, and production service zones  
**Last reviewed:** 13 August 2026

## Context

The pilot needs one dense neighbourhood, an initial category, 3–10 willing vendors, small catalogues, operating hours, and support coverage. Selecting too broad an area would hide delivery and vendor-preparation problems.

## Options under consideration

- Compare two or three pin-code clusters and select one dense cohort: more research, but strongest operational evidence and lowest delivery complexity.
- Select a familiar neighbourhood immediately: faster and cheaper, but risks biased demand and weak vendor coverage.
- Delay selection: avoids premature commitment, but blocks the pilot and all real demand evidence.

## Required evidence

- Candidate city, timezone, pin-code clusters, category, and excluded areas/categories.
- Synthetic vendor identifiers for 3–10 willing vendors and a 20–50 item catalogue summary per vendor.
- Service hours, support coverage, delivery feasibility, and named operational responsibilities.

## Validation-only assumptions

The documented 3–5 km pilot radius, category examples, vendor counts, and catalogue sizes are validation boundaries, not permanent production policy.

## Recommendation

Use Jaipur in `Asia/Kolkata` with candidate test clusters `302020`, `302021`, and `302019`. Begin with nine fictional fruit-and-vegetable vendors and a 20-entry synthetic catalogue per vendor. Keep all activity internal and prohibit real identities, addresses, payments, notifications, or deliveries.

## Decision and approval

**Decision:** Approved for internal company testing only. Candidate zones are Mansarovar (`302020`), Vaishali Nagar (`302021`), and Shyam Nagar (`302019`). The first category is fruit and vegetables with synthetic vendors `FV-01` through `FV-09`. Test service and support hours are Monday–Saturday, 09:00–19:00 IST.  
**Approved by:** Project owner  
**Approval date:** 13 August 2026  
**Reasons:** The scope exercises local produce, variable-weight, availability, substitution, fulfilment, and support workflows without enabling a real marketplace.

## Consequences

This approval defines internal fixtures and scripted operations only. It does not recruit, invite, verify, or publish any real vendor; authorize real personal data; prove serviceability; set production hours; or permit public ordering. Moving beyond internal testing requires reopening this decision with protected real-world evidence and all applicable production gates.
