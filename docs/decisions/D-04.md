# D-04 — Provider simulators and future real-provider gate

**Status:** APPROVED — LOCAL SIMULATORS ONLY  
**Accountable owner:** Finance Lead with Engineering Lead  
**Due date:** 4 September 2026  
**Blocks:** No internal adapter work; real provider use and external side effects remain blocked  
**Last reviewed:** 14 August 2026

## Context

The marketplace eventually needs real OTP, payment, payout, maps, push, SMS, email, and object-storage services. Current company development must remain free, local, and synthetic while exercising complete provider behaviour.

## Approved decision

- Use deterministic local provider simulators and protected outboxes.
- Cover success, decline, timeout, unknown, malformed response, invalid signature, replay, duplicate, outage, and reconciliation mismatch.
- Keep COD disabled.
- Use no sandbox or production credential during the local-only phase.
- Save durable receipts, deduplicate callbacks, use idempotency keys, and reconcile fictional statements.
- Follow [the simulator contract](../providers/simulator-contracts.md) and [ADR-002](../architecture/ADR-002-provider-simulators-and-test-money.md).

## Future real-provider requirement

Before production, select providers using capability, compliance, cost, support, exit, regional, statement, retry, timeout, and reconciliation evidence. Run the common contract suite against provider sandboxes, then controlled real cases with company accounts, followed by a limited production canary. Credentials must come through approved secret management.

Real customer phone numbers, addresses, payments, bank accounts, and messages remain prohibited until privacy, legal, security, finance, operations, and release gates approve them.

## Consequences

Phase 2 may build provider-neutral interfaces and local fixtures. Passing simulator tests does not authorize or certify a real provider.
