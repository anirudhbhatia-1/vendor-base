## Context

Development needs balanced examples for fees, earnings, tips, tax fields, rounding, cancellations, refunds, settlements, payouts, and reconciliation. The company has no pilot evidence or qualified review supporting permanent production amounts.

## Approved internal policy

- Customer delivery fee: ₹15 including 2 route km, then ₹8 per additional route km proportionally, once per vendor order.
- Outer synthetic route boundary: 40 km.
- Vendor platform fee: ₹5 for a successfully completed vendor order; zero for full refund or failure.
- Partner outbound and authorized return earnings: independently versioned copies of the synthetic distance formula.
- Waiting earning: first 5 minutes included, then ₹1 per completed minute, capped automatically at ₹10.
- Tips: 100% to the delivery partner.
- Tax: explicit zero in normal synthetic records, plus positive fictional fixtures; no tax-exempt claim.
- Money: integer paise, deterministic allocation, and append-only balanced ledger entries.
- Cancellation/refund: customer-protective before pickup; controlled return after pickup; component-specific partial refund after successful delivery.
- Eligibility: 24 hours after completion when no affected hold exists.
- Fake payout batch: daily at 18:00 Asia/Kolkata.
- Reconciliation: checkpointed, paginated, idempotent, and exception-driven.

The authoritative rules and examples are in [test-financial-policy.md](../finance/test-financial-policy.md).

## Production boundary

Every amount and rule above is synthetic and changeable through a new version. Historical test orders retain their original version. Production fees, partner contracts, taxes, cancellation liability, refunds, settlement schedules, payouts, and approval thresholds require evidence plus Product, Finance, Operations, legal/tax, and other applicable approval.

## Consequences

Phase 2 can test complete financial paths and conservation invariants. It may not charge a person, pay a vendor or partner, issue a legal tax document, or describe these values as a market launch price.
