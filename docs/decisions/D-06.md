# D-06 — Delivery operating model

**Status:** OPEN  
**Accountable owner:** Operations Lead  
**Due date:** 18 September 2026  
**Blocks:** Phase 0.5 and Phase 9 delivery-partner production launch  
**Last reviewed:** 13 August 2026

## Context

The project must decide how delivery partners are engaged, insured, paid, protected, supported, and held accountable without inventing employment, safety, or liability policy.

## Options under consideration

- Small directly managed pilot cohort: strongest training and operational learning, but higher coordination effort and limited scale.
- Third-party delivery provider: faster coverage, but less control over custody, partner experience, support, data, and unit economics.
- Vendor-only delivery/pickup: simpler platform operations, but weaker shared-delivery value and inconsistent coverage.

## Required evidence

- Applicable legal review of engagement model, insurance, safety, working conditions, and incident responsibility.
- Pay, waiting, return, dispute, training, emergency, equipment, support, custody, and active-order continuity policies.

## Validation-only assumptions

Using one or two known partners in a concierge pilot does not establish a production employment or contracting policy.
