# D-06 — Internal delivery testing and future operating-model gate

**Status:** APPROVED — COMPANY TEST COHORT ONLY
**Accountable owner:** Operations Lead  
**Due date:** 18 September 2026  
**Blocks:** No synthetic delivery-flow testing; real recruitment, delivery, contracting, insurance, and production launch remain blocked
**Last reviewed:** 17 August 2026

## Approved internal decision

Use authorized company team members or specifically approved known testers with synthetic customers, vendors, orders, routes, proofs, earnings, and payouts. Provide a safety/data briefing, prohibit unsafe time pressure, allow immediate test stoppage, and exercise assignment, wait, reconnect, custody, failure, return, proof, and dispute paths.

This does not classify a tester as a production employee or contractor and does not approve public recruitment, real delivery, real payout, or an insurance model.

## Production gate

Legal and Operations must approve engagement, insurance, safety, working conditions, background checks, equipment, training, emergency response, support, liability, custody, pay, dispute, and active-order continuity before real delivery. See [the governance and transition policy](../governance/internal-safety-and-production-transition.md) and production row [`OPS-01`](../production/test-to-production-replacement-register.md).

## Consequences

Phase 2 may build and test delivery workflows. Production partner onboarding and platform-managed delivery remain blocked.
