# D-07 — Verification and document policy

**Status:** OPEN  
**Accountable owner:** Compliance Lead  
**Due date:** 25 September 2026  
**Blocks:** Phase 0.5, Phase 5 vendor publication, and Phase 9 partner publication  
**Last reviewed:** 13 August 2026

## Context

Vendor and partner eligibility requires a lawful, category-aware policy for document collection, review, expiry, reverification, restriction, and appeal.

## Options under consideration

- Risk- and category-based requirements: proportionate and adaptable, but needs careful rule ownership and reviewer training.
- One broad document checklist: simpler to administer, but may over-collect personal data or miss category-specific obligations.
- Outsourced verification: may reduce manual work, but adds provider cost, data sharing, false-result handling, and appeal dependencies.

## Required evidence

- Lawful document requirements and purpose per vendor/partner category.
- Permitted viewers, secure storage, expiry/grace effects, reverification, active-obligation handling, rejection/restriction reasons, and appeal SLAs.

## Validation-only assumptions

No real identity, bank, insurance, licence, or business document belongs in source control or synthetic fixtures.
