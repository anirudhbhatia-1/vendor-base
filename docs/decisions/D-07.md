# D-07 — Synthetic verification-document lifecycle

**Status:** APPROVED — FICTIONAL DOCUMENTS ONLY
**Accountable owner:** Compliance Lead  
**Due date:** 25 September 2026  
**Blocks:** No lifecycle testing; real requirements and production publication remain blocked
**Last reviewed:** 17 August 2026

## Approved internal decision

Use risk- and category-based fictional document types with synthetic files. Test draft, submission, review, approval, rejection, expiry, revocation, reminder, restriction, reverification, second review, and appeal. Expiry may block new publication or work under the active policy but never abandons accepted orders, custody, returns, refunds, settlements, or payouts.

## Production gate

Compliance, legal, and privacy reviewers must approve each real document's lawful purpose, actor/category, viewers, processor, expiry effect, grace/reverification rules, restriction, and appeal path. No real identity, licence, bank, insurance, vehicle, business, or other verification document is approved here.

The full test lifecycle is in [the governance and transition policy](../governance/internal-safety-and-production-transition.md). Production replacement requirements are in rows [`DATA-04`](../production/test-to-production-replacement-register.md), [`PRV-09`](../production/test-to-production-replacement-register.md), and [`OPS-06`](../production/test-to-production-replacement-register.md).

## Consequences

Phase 2 may implement the generic document state machine and synthetic fixtures. Production vendor or partner publication remains blocked.
