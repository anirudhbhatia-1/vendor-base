# D-08 — Internal privacy, access, and synthetic retention

**Status:** APPROVED — SYNTHETIC DATA ONLY
**Accountable owner:** Privacy Lead  
**Due date:** 2 October 2026  
**Blocks:** No privacy-control testing; real personal-data processing remains blocked
**Last reviewed:** 17 August 2026

## Approved internal decision

- Use the data-class synthetic retention schedule and bounded automatic cleanup.
- Limit sensitive access by role, capability, resource, assignment/case, stage, and purpose.
- Use English/Hindi purpose-specific notices and separate optional choices.
- Collect no partner location while offline, coarse location while available, and adaptive precise location only for active work.
- Support verified export, correction, deletion/anonymization, explicit holds, automatic resumption, and appeal.
- Keep pharmacy and prescription processing disabled.

The authoritative matrix, periods, flows, and tests are in [the governance and transition policy](../governance/internal-safety-and-production-transition.md). The separate [test-to-production replacement register](../production/test-to-production-replacement-register.md) is the canonical inventory of synthetic/local components and their production gates.

## Production gate

Real personal data remains prohibited until Privacy, legal, and Security approve the inventory, purpose/lawful basis, notice, consent, rights, processors, cross-boundary handling, retention, deletion, incident response, and user-facing language under then-current law.

## Consequences

Phase 2 may build complete privacy machinery using synthetic records. This is not an approved production privacy notice or retention schedule.
