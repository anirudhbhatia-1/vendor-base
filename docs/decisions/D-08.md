# D-08 — Privacy and retention

**Status:** OPEN  
**Accountable owner:** Privacy Lead  
**Due date:** 2 October 2026  
**Blocks:** Phase 0.5 and Phase 4 use of production personal data  
**Last reviewed:** 13 August 2026

## Context

The marketplace may process phone numbers, addresses, locations, identity documents, bank details, support evidence, device data, and financial records. Each class needs a lawful purpose, limited viewers, consent where required, retention, deletion, and incident handling.

## Options under consideration

- Data-class schedule with minimum necessary retention: strongest privacy and deletion clarity, but requires careful mapping and enforcement.
- One general retention period: easier to describe, but likely unlawful or operationally unsuitable across different data classes.
- Provider-default retention: lowest initial effort, but unacceptable because ownership, deletion, location, and evidence access become unclear.

## Required evidence

- Data inventory with purpose, lawful basis/consent, collection point, viewers, precision, storage, processors, retention, deletion, export, and incident response.
- Special review for identity, location, bank, payment, evidence, and any future health data.

## Validation-only assumptions

Synthetic data may be used for testing. Pharmacy and prescription data remain disabled and require a separate compliance release.
