# D-09 — Support and approval thresholds

**Status:** OPEN  
**Accountable owner:** Operations Lead  
**Due date:** 9 October 2026  
**Blocks:** Phase 0.5 and Phase 11 manual refunds, restrictions, and configuration publication  
**Last reviewed:** 13 August 2026

## Context

Support teams need staffed hours, service targets, role permissions, escalation routes, and independent approval thresholds for sensitive or high-value actions.

## Options under consideration

- Risk-tiered queues and thresholds: directs attention to urgent work and limits authority, but requires training, coverage, and careful configuration.
- One general support queue: cheaper initially, but unsafe for emergencies, money discrepancies, custody, privacy incidents, and expiring decisions.
- Outsourced first-line support: expands coverage, but adds cost, data-access, training, escalation, and quality-control requirements.

## Required evidence

- Role/capability matrix, staffed hours, queue owners, P1/P2/P3 definitions, response/resolution targets, escalation and handoff paths.
- Maker-checker actions, monetary/risk thresholds, emergency authority, expiry, appeal, audit, and active-obligation safeguards.

## Validation-only assumptions

Default timers in `implementation.md` may support simulations but are not an approved staffing or service-level commitment.
