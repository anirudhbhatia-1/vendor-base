## Approved internal decision

- Staffed test hours: Monday–Saturday, 09:00–19:00 `Asia/Kolkata`.
- P0 immediate/5-minute, P1 15-minute, P2 4-staffed-hour, and P3 next-staffed-day acknowledgement targets.
- Name a handoff owner for open safety, custody, privacy, or payment work at closing.
- Use scoped, reversible, audited incident controls and safe-resumption checks.
- Permit an authorized support lead to execute a synthetic refund/adjustment up to ₹500 after impact preview.
- Require another Finance approver above ₹500 and a separate authorized approver for all other protected actions listed in the governance policy.
- Reject self-approval, expired approval, reused approval, and execution after previewed state changes.

See [the governance and transition policy](../governance/internal-safety-and-production-transition.md) and production rows [`FIN-08`](../production/test-to-production-replacement-register.md), [`OPS-02`](../production/test-to-production-replacement-register.md), and [`OPS-03`](../production/test-to-production-replacement-register.md).

## Production gate

Staffing, public hours, service targets, monetary/risk thresholds, training, emergency authority, fraud controls, and escalation require Operations, Finance, Security, and applicable legal approval.

## Consequences

Phase 2 may build queues, roles, maker-checker, handoff, incident, and audit tests. No real monetary authority or service promise is created.
