# D-10 — Recovery objectives

**Status:** OPEN  
**Accountable owner:** Engineering Lead  
**Due date:** 16 October 2026  
**Blocks:** Phase 0.5 and Phase 13 public launch  
**Last reviewed:** 13 August 2026

## Context

The project needs approved limits for acceptable data loss and recovery time, plus tested backup, restore, incident escalation, and communication procedures.

## Options under consideration

- Managed backups with tested point-in-time recovery: lower operational difficulty and strong recovery options, but ongoing provider cost and dependency.
- Scheduled backup snapshots: cheaper and simpler, but potentially more data loss and slower recovery.
- Self-managed recovery system: maximum control, but highest engineering, security, testing, and on-call burden.

## Required evidence

- Approved recovery point objective (RPO) and recovery time objective (RTO) per critical capability.
- Backup scope, encryption, isolation, retention, access, restore procedure, provider failure plan, and non-production restore-test evidence.
- Incident roles, severity, escalation, communication, reconciliation, and safe-resumption policy.

Architecture availability targets and provider capabilities are recommendations until this record and D-03 are approved.
