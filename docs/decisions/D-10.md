# D-10 — Recovery targets and synthetic restore drills

**Status:** APPROVED TARGETS — PRODUCTION CAPABILITY UNPROVEN
**Accountable owner:** Engineering Lead  
**Due date:** 16 October 2026  
**Blocks:** No synthetic restore testing; public launch remains blocked until targets are proven
**Last reviewed:** 17 August 2026

## Approved decision

- Critical order, money, refund, payout, and active-custody data targets no logical loss for acknowledged writes, a backup point no older than 5 minutes, and usable recovery within 1 hour.
- Identity, vendor, catalogue, inventory, support, and configuration target a 1-hour recovery point and 4-hour recovery time.
- Rebuildable search, report, and analytics data target 24 hours for both.
- Local development uses isolated synthetic backup/restore drills after migration changes and on the regular test schedule.
- A restore must verify schema, constraints, PostGIS, objects, ledger balance, permissions, outbox replay, reconciliation, and safe resumption.
- Restore artifacts expire after 7 days.

The detailed synthetic drill is in [the governance and transition policy](../governance/internal-safety-and-production-transition.md). Production replacement and proof requirements are in rows [`INF-08`](../production/test-to-production-replacement-register.md) and [`REL-05`](../production/test-to-production-replacement-register.md).

## Production gate

Hosting, replication, encryption, backup isolation/retention, regional/provider failure, secrets, queue recovery, monitoring, cost, incident command, and restore evidence remain unresolved. Public launch cannot proceed until a shared non-production restore proves the approved targets.

## Consequences

Phase 2 may build recovery scripts and tests. This record does not claim that the current local or future hosted system has achieved production recovery capability.
