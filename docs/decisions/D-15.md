# D-15 — Staging implementation and phase re-audit

**Recorded:** 6 September 2026
**Status:** Owner-approved implementation direction; activation and public launch remain gated
**Basis:** Owner's Production-Shaped Customer Marketplace Improvement Plan and request to synchronize the documents and audit phases from the beginning.

## Authority and boundaries

This workstream supersedes historical local-only implementation restrictions in D-04/D-12 and BUILD-01. D-13 remains evidence of database-only remote development. Earlier decisions are preserved as history; they do not negate subsequent owner authorization to implement the marketplace. No synthetic pilot becomes market validation through this update.

Approved staging targets: Supabase PostgreSQL/PostGIS and Storage; NestJS/Fastify API; active Valkey/BullMQ workers; MSG91; Razorpay Test Mode; Mappls; FCM and Web Push. Credentials, accounts, operational approvals and deployed availability are not assumed. Public production, spending, real customer recruitment and legal/compliance approvals remain separate gates.

Staging uses separate migration and least-privilege runtime identities through the session pooler with `sslmode=verify-full`. D-13's permissive TLS rule is historical; D-14's production transaction-pooler proposal does not govern staging. Production topology remains separately reviewed. Clients never access marketplace tables directly.

Fake adapters are allowed only in local development and automated tests. Staging requires configured approved integration/sandbox adapters, production approved live adapters; invalid combinations must fail startup. This is a target requirement, not a claim about current runtime enforcement. Real SMS tests require approved templates and allowlisted consenting recipients; do not assume MSG91 offers a free isolated sandbox.

## Clients and scope

- Four separate PWAs and four separate Android apps: customer, vendor, partner, admin. Shared contracts do not share authorization scopes.
- Customer PWA targets Workers/OpenNext with same-origin BFF, HttpOnly/Secure/SameSite cookies and CSRF protection. Android uses Expo Router and SecureStore. Other PWAs are not automatically migrated by this workstream.
- Dedicated customer routes cover home, categories, stores, search, product/store detail, favourites, addresses, cart, checkout, orders/detail/tracking/receipt, refunds, support conversations, notifications, sessions and privacy.
- Pickup, approved vendor delivery and platform delivery have explicit workflows. Moving customer maps, masked calling, POS sync, pharmacy, COD and automatic consolidation remain deferred/gated.
- Supabase Storage separates public approved catalogue assets and private quarantined evidence. Scanning/moderation precedes publication; signed private URLs are short-lived and ownership-scoped.
- Customers see final payable totals and customer-facing items, taxes, discounts and charges, not internal fee formulas or margins. Vendors see contracted deductions.

## Canonical checkout chronology

1. Validate cart version, quote, serviceability and inventory. In one PostgreSQL transaction persist holds, checkout group, vendor child-order drafts, payment attempt, allocations/ledger intents and outbox records. No gateway network call is inside this transaction.
2. Create the provider order against the durable attempt outside the transaction; persist provider references. Unknown results enter reconciliation, not a new charge attempt.
3. Customer completes hosted/tokenized checkout. Verify callback signatures and durably ingest signed webhooks. Reconcile provider state; browser success alone never marks payment complete.
4. Staging baseline confirms capture before releasing child orders to vendors. Capture, fulfilment completion, settlement eligibility and payout are different events. Do not assume repeated partial captures against one authorization. Provider behavior must pass adapter tests before activation.
5. Failed/abandoned/expired payment releases holds. Late payment must not revive expired inventory or oversell: reconcile and refund if valid fulfilment cannot be established.
6. Child cancellation/reduced weight refunds only its allocation. Higher totals require explicit customer approval and confirmed top-up within a newly approved maximum. Refusal follows remove-line/cancellation policy, never a silent increase.
7. Settlement eligibility follows fulfilment and the applicable dispute window. Version receipt corrections; never rewrite ledger history. Payment collection does not establish payout-provider readiness.

## Required connected workflows

- Admin creates vendor/partner identity and scoped membership, sends an expiring one-use invitation, and the recipient verifies identity and establishes credentials. Admins cannot retrieve permanent plaintext passwords. Onboarding approval and login membership are separate controls.
- Unpublished stores permit authorized internal test orders only. Test orders never enter public discovery, live payouts or public metrics.
- Guest cart merge uses a stable idempotency key, vendor/location/variant identity and deterministic quantity rules. Report limit/availability conflicts; clear guest state only after acknowledgement.
- Addresses support manual/geocoded entry and permission fallback. Platform partners receive exact customer address only after verified custody. Vendor self-delivery staff receive only delivery-stage access. Operational exact-address access ends with the task.
- Rotation, individual/all-device revocation, account switching, recovery, phone change, export and deletion require behavior tests. Check active obligations, retention and legal holds before deletion.

## Evidence and release gates

Track phase steps as NOT_REVIEWED, PARTIAL, VERIFIED_LOCAL, VERIFIED_STAGING or BLOCKED, with command, result, environment and residual gaps. File presence/source-text assertions do not prove runtime behavior. Scope approval is not a test result.

Staging targets: discovery/search p95 <500 ms; transactional API p95 <750 ms with provider wait separately measured; 1,000 concurrent browsing users and 100 checkout confirmations/minute; zero oversells/duplicate charges; backlog recovery after provider outage. Targets are not achieved measurements or unlimited free-tier promises.

Public release additionally requires security/compliance review, owned alerts/support, backup restoration, disaster recovery and capacity approval. Payout provider and production financial policies remain explicit unclosed gates.
