# D-17 — Mapbox for road distance and travel time in the synthetic environments

**Recorded:** 20 September 2026
**Status:** Owner-approved provider direction; synthetic environments only; production routing, spending beyond the free tier and real-customer coordinates remain gated
**Basis:** Owner request, 20 September 2026, to give every surface real road distance and time "so that distance and time accuracy is there", choosing Mapbox over Google Maps Platform and a self-hosted OSRM.

## Authority and boundaries

D-05 prices the customer delivery fee and the partner's earning per *route* kilometre. D-15 listed Mappls as the staging maps target and assumed no credentials. Until this decision every distance in the system was a straight line (`ST_Distance`), so every fee, earning and "distance to store" was short by whatever the roads add, and no surface promised a travel time at all — the checkout's "Arrives in 45–75 minutes" was fixed copy.

Selected:

- **Provider:** Mapbox Directions and Matrix APIs, `driving` profile (OpenStreetMap road network). Mapbox replaces Mappls as the maps target for the synthetic environments; Mappls or another Indian provider remains open for production under the register (PRV-04).
- **Where the road replaces the line:** the customer delivery fee and the promise at the quote; the partner offer's distance, travel time and expected earning; the partner's settled trip earning; store discovery ordering and the maximum-distance filter; the shop's own self-delivery leg. Each reads the route the quote stored, so the distance a rider is shown and paid on is the distance the customer was charged for.
- **Fallback:** when no provider is configured (local development, automated tests) or the provider fails, times out or finds no road, the route is the straight line times a road factor of 1.3 at 18 km/h, and it is labelled `ESTIMATE` wherever it is stored or shown. A deployment must either route on real roads or acknowledge itself as synthetic, the same rule the fake OTP adapter follows.
- **Cache:** routes are kept for a day by rounded coordinate pair (`route_cache`, migration 0085), so one order's quote, offer and settlement ask the provider once.
- **Credential:** the Mapbox access token is injected as `MAPBOX_ACCESS_TOKEN` from the environment store only, never committed, never sent to a browser. Coordinates sent to Mapbox are synthetic in every environment this decision covers.

## Why Mapbox

Google Maps Platform has the best Indian coverage and live traffic but needs a billing account and carries a higher privacy bar for production. A self-hosted OSRM is free and offline but needs a map extract and a fourth container app in the Azure environment. Mapbox's free tier (100,000 Directions requests a month) covers the synthetic environments without spending, one token configures every surface, and the adapter is thin enough to replace.

## What remains gated

- Production routing needs the register's PRV-04 evidence: address quality, route accuracy, serviceability, timeout, quota, cost, privacy, cache and fallback tests, with Engineering, Privacy and Operations sign-off.
- Real customer coordinates may not be sent to any provider until that sign-off exists.
- Spending: the free tier only; a budget alert before any paid tier.
- An ETA model beyond preparation time plus road time (the promise's quarter-hour slack is a stated estimate, not a service level).

## Decision and approval

**Decision:** Mapbox is the routing provider for the synthetic environments; the labelled estimate is the fallback and the test provider.
**Approved by:** Project Owner, in the working session of 20 September 2026
**Reasons:** Free tier covers synthetic use; one token; replaceable adapter; D-05's route semantics honoured for the first time.

## Consequences

Fees and earnings rise by the road factor; the quote's promise becomes a calculation; the register's PRV-04 row moves from `BLOCKED` to `PATH_PENDING_2_1` with the adapter implemented and the token to be configured per environment.
