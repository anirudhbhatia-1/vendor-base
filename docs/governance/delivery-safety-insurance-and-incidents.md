# Delivery safety, insurance, and incident ownership

Partners must have an approved synthetic identity, vehicle, training, emergency-contact and insurance reference before company-test availability. Production insurance requirements remain a legal/provider decision.

The partner controls immediate personal safety and may stop work without penalty. Operations owns custody routing and customer/vendor communication. The incident commander owns cross-team decisions. Trust and safety owns evidence and restrictions. Finance owns monetary correction; support cannot alter ledger entries.

Accident, medical emergency, threat, unsafe location, prohibited goods, missing package and vehicle failure use structured exception codes. Emergency events bypass routine support, preserve the last safe custody state, hide unnecessary personal data, and open an incident with owner, review time and recovery checklist. Return destinations must be server-authorized; vendor refusal routes to an alternate safe point and operations review. No person should continue a delivery to satisfy an SLA when unsafe.
