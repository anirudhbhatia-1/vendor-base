# Support service levels and authority

**Hours:** company test desk, Monday–Saturday 09:00–19:00 Asia/Kolkata, as approved in [D-09](../decisions/D-09.md) (an earlier version of this file said 08:00–20:00). Outside-hours critical events page the on-call operations lead.

| Severity (D-09 label) | Example | First response | Update cadence | Resolution owner |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Critical (P0) | Safety threat, custody loss, ledger conservation break | 5 minutes | 15 minutes | Incident commander |
| High (P1) | Live order blocked, payment unknown, return refused | 15 minutes | 30 minutes | Operations lead |
| Medium (P2) | Refund/payout mismatch, onboarding verification | 4 staffed hours | Daily | Queue owner |
| Low (P3) | Profile/catalogue question | Next staffed day | Every 2 staffed days | Support agent |

Refunds and corrections must use maker/checker approval. Support may communicate and collect evidence but cannot rewrite order, custody, ledger, or audit history. Escalation is mandatory at 75% of the response deadline. Closed cases receive quality review samples; reopening retains the original case timeline.
