# Support service levels and authority

**Hours:** company test desk, Monday–Saturday 08:00–20:00 Asia/Kolkata. Outside-hours critical events page the on-call operations lead.

| Severity | Example | First response | Update cadence | Resolution owner |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Critical | Safety threat, custody loss, ledger conservation break | 5 minutes | 15 minutes | Incident commander |
| High | Live order blocked, payment unknown, return refused | 15 minutes | 30 minutes | Operations lead |
| Medium | Refund/payout mismatch, onboarding verification | 4 business hours | Daily | Queue owner |
| Low | Profile/catalogue question | 1 business day | Every 2 business days | Support agent |

Refunds and corrections must use maker/checker approval. Support may communicate and collect evidence but cannot rewrite order, custody, ledger, or audit history. Escalation is mandatory at 75% of the response deadline. Closed cases receive quality review samples; reopening retains the original case timeline.
